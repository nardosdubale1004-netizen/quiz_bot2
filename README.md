## 🏆 How You Earn Points

Every correct answer is scored the same transparent way, every time:

```
YOUR POINTS  =  Difficulty  ×  Speed Bonus  ×  Challenge Bonus  ×  Streak Momentum
```

Four independent, fully-disclosed multipliers. No hidden dice roll — the same inputs always produce the same fair result.

### 🧩 1 · Difficulty — harder questions simply pay more

| Difficulty | Base Points | Design intent |
|:---:|:---:|---|
| 🟢 Easy | **3** | Warm-up material |
| 🟡 Medium | **6** | Core curriculum |
| 🔴 Hard | **12** | Real challenge — worth **4× an easy question** |

> Chasing volume on easy questions will never out-earn genuinely tackling hard material.

### ⚡ 2 · Speed Bonus — reward attention, not luck

Everyone who answers quickly gets rewarded — not just the first three people. No race against strangers, no anxiety.

| You answer within | Multiplier |
|:---:|:---:|
| ⚡ First 60 seconds | **×1.5** |
| 🏃 First 5 minutes | **×1.2** |
| 🚶 Anytime after | **×1.0** *(still full credit — nothing is ever lost by being slow)* |

### 🎯 3 · Challenge Bonus — bravery pays, farming doesn't

Every question carries a grade tag, compared against **your own** registered grade:

| Situation | Multiplier |
|---|:---:|
| 🚀 You tackle a question *above* your grade | **×1.5** |
| ⚖️ Question *matches* your grade | **×1.0** |
| 🐢 You answer *well below* your grade | **×0.3** *(still counts — just isn't farmable)* |

### 🔥 4 · Streak Momentum — consistency compounds

See the [Daily Streaks](#-daily-streaks--your-momentum-engine) section below for the full breakdown.

---

### 🧮 Worked Example — same effort, radically different payout

> **Case A:** A student answers a **Hard**, above-grade question in **45 seconds**, riding a **7-day streak**:
> ```
> 12 (hard) × 1.5 (lightning speed) × 1.5 (bravery) × 1.5 (7-day streak) = 40.5 → 40 points
> ```
> **Case B:** An **Easy**, same-grade question, answered slowly, with no streak:
> ```
> 3 × 1.0 × 1.0 × 1.0 = 3 points
> ```
> **The system is built to make growth pay off — deliberately, transparently, every time.**

## 🔥 Daily Streaks — Your Momentum Engine

Answer **at least one question correctly, once a day**, to build your streak. Miss a day → it resets to 1. Consistency beats intensity here.

| Streak Length | Multiplier |
|:---:|:---:|
| Days 1–2 | ×1.0 |
| Days 3–6 | **×1.2** |
| Day 7+ | **×1.5** |

> 💡 A 7-day streak means **every single point you earn is worth 50% more** — for as long as it holds. This is the single highest-leverage habit in the entire system, and it costs nothing but showing up.

## ⚔️ Live Tournaments — Real-Time Showdowns

Beyond the regular daily feed, the channel occasionally hosts **live, synchronized tournaments** — everyone competes on the exact same question, at the exact same time, on a ticking clock.

| Feature | What it means for you |
|---|---|
| 📢 **Announced in advance** | A pinned card shows the subject, topic mix, difficulty spread, and exact global start time (with your local time zone shown too) |
| ⏱️ **Live countdown** | The channel message updates in real time so you always know exactly how long is left |
| 🥇 **Round podium** | The 3 fastest correct answers each round are shown publicly (unless you've opted for private mode — see [Privacy](#-your-privacy--data--read-this-part-carefully)) |
| 🏆 **Tournament-only leaderboard** | Scores are ranked *within that tournament series only* — completely separate from your all-time score, so newcomers compete on a level field |
| 👑 **Grand Champions podium** | At the end of the series, the top individual, top school team, top city, and top country are celebrated together |
| 🔁 **Everything still counts** | Every correct answer in a tournament also adds to your lifetime score, streak, and subject mastery — nothing is "just for fun," but nothing regular is lost either |

> Miss a live round? No penalty whatsoever — your streak and lifetime stats are completely unaffected. Tournaments are a bonus event layer, not a requirement.

## 🤝 Invite Friends, Earn Together

Your personal referral link is always available via `/invite`.

- When someone joins through your link and **answers correctly**, you earn a **small share of the points they earn** — real mentorship, rewarded proportionally, not a flat bounty for signing people up.
- It works **two levels deep**: if your invitee later invites someone else, you still earn a smaller trickle-down share from that second-level student's correct answers too.
- **There is no reward just for recruiting.** Every referral point traces back to someone actually learning — mass-inviting inactive accounts earns you nothing, because inactive accounts earn nothing to share.

## 🔄 Why the Question Feed Never Feels Repetitive

You will never see the same question again within **at least three weeks** — a hard rule, not a suggestion. Behind the scenes, every new post is chosen to:

- ✅ **Never repeat** anything shown recently
- ✅ **Balance subjects** so no single topic dominates the feed for long stretches
- ✅ **Balance difficulty** so you get a healthy mix of easy, medium, and hard
- ✅ **Stay a little unpredictable** — so the feed feels alive, not mechanically patterned

You don't have to do anything for this — it's automatic, and it's the same promise on day one or day one thousand.

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Can I lose points?**
No. Points only ever go up. An incorrect answer simply earns zero for that question — it never subtracts.

**Q: What happens if I answer a question twice?**
Only your **first attempt** counts for scoring. Later views show your original answer, locked in — keeps the leaderboard fair for everyone.

**Q: Is this free?**
Completely. No ads, no payments, no premium tier locking features behind a paywall.

**Q: I'm not a strong student — will this still be useful for me?**
Yes — the grade-challenge system specifically rewards attempting things above your comfort zone, and the subject mastery view helps you see exactly where to focus rather than feeling generally behind.

**Q: My streak broke because I forgot one day. Is that it?**
Your streak resets to 1, but your lifetime score, mastery rank, and subject progress are completely unaffected. One missed day costs you nothing permanent.

**Q: Can I use this if I'm not in a school program?**
Yes. Study Alliance teams are entirely optional — everything else works identically whether you're solo or on a team, and personal city/country settings power your local leaderboards either way.

**Q: Does this work outside my country?**
Yes — the bot has no regional restrictions. Set your city/country in `/profile`, and you'll appear on the right local leaderboards no matter where you are in the world.
